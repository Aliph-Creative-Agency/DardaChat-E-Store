Three sections cannot be completed, and nothing can be priced with confidence, until we have the following. Two of them (the first and third) have long lead times outside both our control and yours, so they are worth starting this week even before you have read the rest.

5. **Data protection.** Which data protection law the business is subject to decides how we handle customer data, breach notification and deletion requests. If you have legal advice on this, please share it; if not, tell us and we will propose a basis for you to confirm.
