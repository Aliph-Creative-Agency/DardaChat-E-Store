## [BLOCKING] FR-AI-011 / FR-AI-016

- Problem: FR-AI-016 is the document's only prompt-injection requirement and it quarantines exactly one thing: 'customer message content'. But FR-AI-011 opens a second, more dangerous route into the model's context - staff promote entries from the unanswered-question queue into the retrieval corpus, and the contents of that queue are customer-authored text. An attacker asks a question crafted to read like a policy statement, it lands in the queue as unanswered, a busy one-person operation promotes it, and from that moment FR-AI-002 retrieves it as trusted grounding for every future customer. The injection is now persistent, cross-customer, and specifically laundered through the mechanism the SRS designates as authoritative. Nothing requires the promoted answer to be staff-authored rather than customer-quoted, nothing requires review or approval before it enters the corpus, and no requirement states that retrieved content is data rather than instruction. FR-AI-016's acceptance criterion ('A curated prompt-injection set') will not catch this either, because it exercises the message channel, and the section 7 instrument for 4.14 lists 'injection' without distinguishing the two paths. Given FR-AI-006 grants five tools and FR-AI-007 exposes order data inside an authenticated session, a poisoned corpus entry has real reach.
- Evidence: FR-AI-011: 'The back office shall present transcripts, an unanswered-question queue, and the means to promote an answer into the retrieval corpus'. FR-AI-016: 'The system shall treat all customer message content as untrusted input; instructions appearing within a customer message shall not alter the assistant instructions, its tool permissions or its escalation behaviour'. FR-AI-002: 'Assistant responses shall be grounded in a retrieval corpus comprising the product catalogue, policy pages and a curated FAQ'.
- Fix: Widen FR-AI-016 from 'customer message content' to 'all content not authored by an authorised user - customer messages and retrieved corpus content alike - shall be treated as data and shall never alter assistant instructions, tool permissions or escalation behaviour.' Amend FR-AI-011 so promotion writes a staff-authored answer, with the customer's question retained only as a non-retrieved label, and require an explicit Owner or Staff approval step recorded in the FR-ACC-012 audit log. Extend FR-AI-016's acceptance criterion to include an injection attempt planted through the unanswered-question queue and promoted, not only one sent as a message.

## [BLOCKING] FR-DAT-006
**FR-DAT-006 immutability makes the FR-DAT-004 erasure duty unbuildable and untestable**

- Problem: Two Must requirements directly contradict each other and the document never states which wins. FR-DAT-006 makes order, payment, stock and audit records append-only, with a criterion that update and delete are refused outright. FR-DAT-004 requires erasure or irreversible anonymisation of the subject's personal data, with a criterion that no query returns identifying data afterwards. Those records hold identifying data: 5.2 stores the order against a customer, FR-ADR-001 captures recipient name, phone, directions and landmark, and both FR-ACC-012 and FR-ORD-003 record 'before and after values' of every edit — so an address correction under FR-ORD-013 preserves the old address in an append-only log permanently. FR-DAT-007 attempts the bridge but demands the retained financial record be reshaped into a non-identifying form, which is exactly the amendment FR-DAT-006 forbids. No requirement excludes the audit log from erasure, and no requirement sets any retention age for it, so a deletion request can neither be honoured nor evidenced.
- Evidence: FR-DAT-006: "Order, payment, stock and audit records shall be immutable once written; correction shall occur by compensating entry, never by amendment | Update and delete against these tables are refused". FR-DAT-004: "the system shall erase or irreversibly anonymise the subject personal data | After deletion, no query returns identifying data for the subject." FR-DAT-007: "Deletion shall retain the financial record required for accounting, in a form that no longer identifies the individual".
- Fix: Separate the ledger from the identity rather than arbitrating between the two rules. Require order, payment and stock rows to reference Customer and Address by key and to hold no name, phone or address inline, so erasure becomes a single overwrite outside the immutable set. Require the audit log and order history to record field identifiers plus masked or hashed values for personal-data fields, not raw before/after values. Add a retention age for audit entries and an explicit exclusion, and restate FR-DAT-006 as immutable except for the pseudonymisation performed under FR-DAT-004, which itself writes a compensating entry.

## [BLOCKING] FR-CRT-009
**FR-CRT-009 total lines double-count VAT under VAT-inclusive pricing**

- Problem: AS-06 and FR-CUR-006 make displayed prices VAT-inclusive, so the VAT figure is a component already contained in the subtotal, not an addend. FR-CRT-009's acceptance criterion nevertheless requires the five lines to sum to the charged amount, which is only arithmetically true under VAT-exclusive pricing. Worked example at 16%: subtotal 100 (incl. VAT), discount 10, delivery 20, VAT component 13.79, grand total 110. The criterion's sum gives 123.79. Implemented literally this overcharges every single order by the VAT amount, and the same arithmetic flows straight into the PDF invoice (FR-ORD-006) and the invoice cleared with the tax authority (FR-ORD-019), where a wrong VAT figure is not merely a display bug. Compounding it, no requirement states how a promotion discount (FR-CRT-007) affects the VAT component, whether delivery is VAT-bearing, or how VAT is allocated back to lines — all three are needed before a partial credit note (FR-ORD-021) can be issued for anything.
- Evidence: FR-CRT-009: "Order totals shall display subtotal, discount, delivery, VAT and grand total as separate lines" | criterion: "All five lines render separately and sum to the charged amount to the agora." Against FR-CUR-006: "The system shall apply a configurable VAT rate, shall display prices inclusive of VAT, and shall itemise the VAT component on invoices".
- Fix: Restate FR-CRT-009 as: subtotal (VAT-inclusive) minus discount plus delivery equals grand total, with VAT presented as a non-additive "of which VAT" line. Add a requirement fixing the inclusive-VAT arithmetic: VAT is computed on the final payable amount after discount and delivery, rounded half-up to the agora, and allocated across order lines by largest-remainder so line VAT sums exactly to the header VAT. State explicitly whether delivery carries VAT, and that a discount reduces each line's VAT-inclusive amount pro rata.

## [BLOCKING] FR-ORD-019
**No requirement states when an invoice is issued, or voids one after cancellation**

- Problem: Electronic invoicing is confirmed required (AS-11) and is clearance-based, yet nothing in the document names the event that creates and submits an invoice. Appendix A contains no invoiced state and no invoice side effect on any transition; FR-ORD-006 generates the PDF with no stated trigger; FR-PAY-012 says wallet payments produce an invoice, which implies card and COD orders might not. The COD case breaks on an ordinary Tuesday: if the invoice is issued at COD_CONFIRMED and the parcel is then refused at the door, the order goes DELIVERY_FAILED then CANCELLED, no money ever moved, and FR-ORD-021 issues a credit note only "for every refund" — so a cleared invoice for a sale that never happened stands against the business with no mechanism to withdraw it. - Evidence: FR-ORD-019: "The system shall submit every invoice electronically to the tax authority... an order cannot be marked invoiced without one." FR-ORD-021: "The system shall issue and submit a credit note for every refund, referencing the original invoice". Appendix A.2: "| DELIVERY_FAILED | CANCELLED | Redelivery declined or abandoned | Refund initiated if PAID |" — no invoice or credit-note side effect anywhere in Appendix A.
- Fix: Add a requirement naming the issuance event per payment method (card/wallet: on PAID; COD: on DELIVERED, when the sale is complete and the cash becomes due), with a packing slip only before that point. Add invoice and credit-note side effects to the affected Appendix A transitions. Restate FR-ORD-021 as: a credit note shall be issued and submitted for every cancellation, return, refund or price correction affecting an order whose invoice has been cleared, whether or not money moves.

## [BLOCKING] FR-PAY-006
**Partial refunds are mandated but have no representation in the order state model**

- Problem: FR-PAY-006 is a Must requiring partial refunds, but REFUNDED in Appendix A is a whole-order terminal state reachable only from RETURNED, and FR-ORD-002's criterion requires that any transition absent from Appendix A be refused. The everyday cases therefore have no path at all: one box of three returned; the delivery charge refunded as goodwill on a late order; a partial credit for a damaged box the customer keeps. Because REFUNDED is terminal with stock effect "Unchanged", any order pushed into it can also never reach COMPLETED — so for a COD order the cash reconciliation (FR-PAY-008) and the return window never close, and the order sits permanently in a state that says the whole payment was reversed when only part of it was. FR-ORD-021's credit note likewise has no way to reference a partial amount or specific lines.
- Evidence: FR-PAY-006: "The system shall support full and partial refunds initiated from the back office, and shall record the refund reference and the initiating user". Appendix A.1: "| REFUNDED | Refund issued against the original payment. Terminal | Unchanged |". Appendix A.2: "| RETURNED | REFUNDED | Refund issued (FR-PAY-006) | Refund reference recorded |". FR-ORD-002 criterion: "Every transition in Appendix A succeeds; every transition absent from it is refused."
- Fix: Model refunds as records against the payment, not as an order state. Add a Refund entity to section 5.2 (amount in agorot, reason, affected order lines, credit note reference, initiating user, timestamp) and let the order remain in DELIVERED, RETURNED or COMPLETED while carrying a refunded total. Either delete REFUNDED and derive the customer-visible label from refunded total versus order total, or restrict it explicitly to the fully-refunded case and add the transitions that reach it from PAID and COD_CONFIRMED.

## [BLOCKING] Appendix A.2
**Payment retry returns an order to PENDING without re-reserving released stock**

- Problem: A.1 releases the reservation when an order enters PAYMENT_FAILED and separately defines PENDING as holding stock "Reserved". A.2's retry transition back to PENDING carries side effect "None". The two tables contradict each other. Under the literal reading the order sits in PENDING claiming a reservation it does not hold and can be paid for stock another customer bought in the interval — a direct oversell, contradicting FR-INV-005 whose whole point is that this cannot happen. Under the charitable reading the system silently re-reserves, but no transition and no failure outcome is defined for the case where the stock has gone. The authors clearly knew this was needed, because DELIVERY_FAILED to PROCESSING does carry "Stock reserved again". A declined card followed by a successful retry minutes later is a weekly event, and with five titles held in units of tens during Ramadan the last unit is genuinely contended. Related: no requirement anywhere sets a reservation expiry, so an order abandoned on the hosted payment page holds its reservation indefinitely — the trigger word "expired" has no defined duration.
- Evidence: Appendix A.1: "| PENDING | Order created; payment not yet resolved | Reserved |" and "| PAYMENT_FAILED | Authorisation declined, timed out, or abandoned | Reservation released |". Appendix A.2: "| PAYMENT_FAILED | PENDING | Customer retries payment | None |". FR-INV-005 criterion: "Fifty concurrent checkouts against one remaining unit produce exactly one order and forty-nine refusals."
- Fix: Give PAYMENT_FAILED to PENDING the side effect "reservation re-acquired", and add an outcome for retry when stock is no longer available (PAYMENT_FAILED to BACKORDERED, or to CANCELLED with a customer message). Add a requirement for a configurable reservation hold — a PENDING order whose payment is unresolved after N minutes moves to PAYMENT_FAILED and releases its reservation. Extend FR-INV-002's acceptance criterion to exercise the decline-then-retry path explicitly.

## [MAJOR] NFR-SEC-002
**NFR-SEC-002's acceptance criterion is materially weaker than the requirement above it**

- Problem: The requirement states an absolute — freedom from an entire class of vulnerability — which is not verifiable by any method in §7 and which no supplier can honestly sign off. The criterion beside it then silently substitutes a different, weaker obligation: no finding at medium severity or above, from whatever scanner is chosen. A low-severity stored XSS or an information disclosure passes the criterion while violating the requirement, so acceptance and the requirement disagree about whether the system is compliant. The criterion is also tool-relative — 'medium' is a scanner's opinion, and switching scanners changes the pass/fail result — which makes the check irreproducible. This matters more than usual here because NFR-SEC-011 stakes PCI DSS SAQ-A eligibility on the application never entering the cardholder data flow, and that claim is only as good as the security verification behind it.
- Evidence: NFR-SEC-002: 'The system shall be free of the OWASP Top 10 vulnerability classes, verified by review and automated scanning before release | Scan and manual review find no finding in any Top 10 category at medium severity or above.'
- Fix: Restate the requirement as the process and threshold that can actually be verified, and let the criterion test that process. For example: 'Before each release the system shall be assessed against the OWASP Application Security Verification Standard Level 1 by review and automated scanning; no finding rated High or Critical shall remain open at release, and any Medium finding shall be recorded with an owner and a remediation date.' Name the scanner and the severity scale in the criterion so the result is reproducible, and add the OWASP Top 10 categories to the §7 group 6.3 instrument as the review checklist rather than as an absolute claim.

FR-AI-003 criterion: 'Across the evaluation set, no response asserts an attribute, price, stock level or delivery promise absent from retrieval or tool output.' FR-AI-009: 'shall offer escalation to a human on WhatsApp when confidence is low' / criterion: 'Escalation offered on low confidence, on request, and after two failed exchanges on one question.'
- Fix: Give FR-AI-013 numbers proportionate to the scale: a fixed set of roughly 60-100 cases spanning the four listed dimensions plus Levantine Arabic per FR-AI-004, a stated pass threshold per dimension (grounding 100 percent, the rest 90 percent), authored jointly and frozen at baseline, with the client named as owner of additions. Soften FR-AI-003's criterion to apply to the frozen set at the stated threshold and add a production sampling requirement, so the bar is honest rather than absolute. Replace 'when confidence is low' in FR-AI-009 with an implementable proxy - escalate when retrieval returns no chunk above a configured relevance score, which is measurable and already implied by FR-AI-015 - and add a requirement stating published support hours, the behaviour outside them (queue the conversation, tell the customer when a human will reply), and a target first-response time.

## [MAJOR] FR-ACC-010
**Two roles are mandated but no permission matrix exists, so FR-ACC-010 cannot be tested**

- Problem: FR-ACC-010's acceptance criterion turns on which endpoints are 'Owner-only', and the document never says. FR-CAT-013 is the only requirement in 856 lines that binds a capability to a named role. Everywhere money or personal data moves, the actor is left vague — FR-PAY-010 lets 'an authorised user' record cash against a COD order, FR-PAY-006 has refunds merely 'initiated from the back office', FR-CRM-007 exports a segment 'subject to role permission', FR-INV-006 is 'restricted to authorised roles', FR-ORD-013 says 'Authorised staff'. Section 7 names a per-role endpoint matrix as the verification instrument for 4.7, but that matrix does not exist, so FR-ACC-010, FR-ACC-011 and NFR-SEC-003 have no testable content and least privilege cannot be reviewed. Section 2.3's statement that the Owner handles 'anything that moves money' is business prose, not a requirement, and it already conflicts with FR-PAY-010's 'authorised user'.
- Evidence: FR-ACC-010: "Back office access shall be governed by two roles, Owner and Staff... | Staff is denied every Owner-only endpoint by direct request with a Staff session cookie." FR-PAY-010: "The system shall allow an authorised user to record cash received against a delivered COD order". Section 7, 4.7 row: "Per-role endpoint matrix by direct request; OTP and MFA cases".
- Fix: Add a one-page permission matrix as an appendix: one row per capability group (catalogue, order edit, refunds, COD cash receipt, pricing, cost and margin data, customer export, stock adjustment, campaigns, user management, DSAR execution) and one column per role. For two roles this is proportionate — it is a single table — and it is what makes FR-ACC-010's criterion executable and NFR-SEC-003's per-role endpoint test meaningful.

## [MAJOR] FR-ACC-004
**OTP rate limiting covers issuance only; nothing caps failed verification attempts**

- Problem: FR-ACC-004 looks like it covers OTP abuse but every clause governs issuance: expiry, single use, and refusal of the eleventh request in an hour. NFR-SEC-004 likewise names 'OTP issuance'. Neither caps incorrect submissions against a live code, and no requirement anywhere states the code length or that it must be drawn from a cryptographically secure source. FR-ACC-002 makes phone plus OTP a complete credential with no password set, so an attacker who requests one code for a target number and then submits guesses is entirely unconstrained — a 4-digit code falls in minutes, and the permitted 10 fresh codes per hour multiply the attempt budget. This is the least expensive hole in the document to close and the one most likely to survive review, precisely because the criterion reads as though rate limiting is already handled.
- Evidence: FR-ACC-004: "OTP codes shall expire within 5 minutes, shall be single-use, and shall be rate-limited per number and per IP address | A code expires at five minutes, fails on second use, and the eleventh request in an hour is refused." NFR-SEC-004: "Authentication endpoints, OTP issuance, password reset and the assistant shall be rate-limited per identity and per address".
- Fix: Extend FR-ACC-004 to require a minimum six-digit code from a cryptographically secure source, invalidation of the code after five incorrect attempts, and a per-number and per-address limit on verification attempts as well as issuance, with the failed-attempt counter surviving the request of a new code. Add 'OTP verification' alongside 'OTP issuance' in NFR-SEC-004, and add a failed-verification case to the 4.7 verification instrument in section 7.

## [MAJOR] FR-ORD-012
**No authorisation path is specified for guest order access despite guest checkout being a Must**

- Problem: Guest checkout is a Must and guest orders carry a null customer_id, yet the only order-visibility requirement grants access to 'a customer' viewing 'their own orders', and FR-AI-007 refuses order lookup outside an authenticated session. Nothing in the document specifies how a guest — likely the majority of COD buyers in this market — sees their order at all. The implementer's obvious fallback is lookup by the FR-ORD-001 reference, which is deliberately optimised for being spoken aloud and is tested only for uniqueness across 10,000 orders and for non-sequentiality; neither property is unguessability, and no rate limit applies to such a lookup. An order record exposes recipient name, phone, free-text directions and landmark (FR-ADR-001) and the map pin (FR-ADR-003), so reference enumeration is a full delivery-address leak for a business whose second dispatch origin is a household address.
- Evidence: FR-CRT-005: "Checkout shall be completable as a guest". 5.2 Order: "customer_id | FK, nullable | Null for guest orders". FR-ORD-012: "A customer shall be able to view the status and history of their own orders, and no others". FR-ORD-001: "a unique, non-sequential, human-communicable reference | References are unique across 10,000 generated orders, non-sequential, and readable aloud without ambiguity."
- Fix: Add a requirement covering guest order access: either a signed, expiring, single-order link delivered inside the FR-MSG-004 transactional messages, or reference plus an OTP to the phone or email recorded on that order. State in FR-ORD-001 that the reference is a communication aid and never an access credential, and add order lookup to the rate-limited surfaces in NFR-SEC-004.

## [MAJOR] FR-AI-017
**Customer PII reaches the model provider with no residency, retention or processing requirement**

- Problem: The redaction requirement covers inbound message content only, and its own carve-out plus the tool set defeat it in the common case: FR-AI-006 and FR-AI-007 give the assistant order status, so a routine 'where is my order' turn legitimately places recipient name, phone and full delivery directions into the provider's context — and nothing in FR-AI-017 constrains tool results entering that context at all. Meanwhile the document pins the hosting region for the datastore and its backups in NFR-PRV-007 but places no equivalent obligation on the named model provider: no processing region, no bar on the content being used for training, no processing agreement, no provider-side retention limit. FR-AI-010 compounds it by retaining transcripts 'subject to the retention policy in section 5.3' when 5.3 sets no period for transcripts — only for Journey data — and the generic mechanism FR-DAT-001 is priority S and therefore deferrable.
- Evidence: FR-AI-017: "shall redact recognisable personal data - phone numbers, email addresses, full postal detail and payment references - from message content before transmission to the model provider, except where the customer has supplied it for an authorised tool lookup within the same turn". 3.3: "Large language model API | Outbound | AI assistant inference | Google Gemini 2.5 Flash". NFR-PRV-007: "The hosting region for the primary datastore and for its backups shall be recorded in the privacy notice".
- Fix: Add a processor requirement alongside NFR-PRV-007: a written processing agreement with the model provider, a named processing region recorded in the privacy notice, provider-side retention set to zero or the shortest available, and an explicit prohibition on the content being used for model training — verified by inspecting the provider configuration. Extend FR-AI-017 to cover tool results entering the model context (pass the order reference and status, not the address and phone), and give assistant transcripts an explicit retention period in 5.3 instead of a reference to a policy that does not cover them.

## [MAJOR] FR-ORD-017
**Redelivery re-reserves stock that is still in the courier's van**

- Problem: At DISPATCHED, On Hand has already been decremented and the parcel physically exists with the courier. DELIVERY_FAILED restores stock only on return to origin, which FR-ORD-017 correctly gates on receipt. But the redelivery transition unconditionally carries "Stock reserved again". For the most common failure by far — nobody home, courier retries tomorrow, the parcel never comes back — this reserves a second unit against On Hand that does not include the parcel already out. The order then reappears in the pick queue and a second box is packed and dispatched while the first is still in the field. With a manual, API-less courier (AS-10) reporting outcomes by phone, distinguishing "held for retry" from "returned to us" is exactly the information the state model needs and does not have; both currently collapse into one DELIVERY_FAILED state with one exit.
- Evidence: Appendix A.1: "| DELIVERY_FAILED | Courier could not deliver, or the customer refused the parcel | Restored on return to origin |". Appendix A.2: "| DELIVERY_FAILED | PROCESSING | Redelivery agreed with the customer | Stock reserved again |". FR-ORD-017: "The system shall restore stock when goods from a failed delivery are received back at the origin Location".
- Fix: Split the failed-delivery outcome into two. Retry with the same parcel still held by the courier: no stock movement, order returns to DISPATCHED, attempt counter incremented under FR-ORD-016. Parcel returned to origin: stock restored per FR-ORD-017, then the order may return to PROCESSING and re-reserve. Make receipt at origin the trigger that distinguishes them, and allow only that second path to re-reserve.

## [MAJOR] FR-PAY-010
**COD refunds have no cash-out path; nothing records money handed back**

- Problem: Every refund mechanism in the document assumes a provider transaction to reverse. FR-PAY-006 records a "refund reference", and REFUNDED is defined as a refund "against the original payment" — but for COD the original payment is cash a courier collected and later remitted (AS-08). FR-PAY-010 records cash coming in, with a receiving party; nothing records cash going out, who paid it, or by what means (cash in person, bank transfer). Worse, the FR-PAY-008 reconciliation report matches cash collected against orders marked delivered, so a refunded COD order continues to count as collected forever and the report will never balance against the till. Given that COD is expected to carry most launch volume and card payment is not yet onboarded at all, a returned COD order is the ordinary refund case, not the edge case.
- Evidence: FR-PAY-010: "The system shall allow an authorised user to record cash received against a delivered COD order, capturing amount, date and receiving party". Appendix A.1: "| REFUNDED | Refund issued against the original payment. Terminal | Unchanged |". FR-PAY-008: "a COD reconciliation report matching cash collected against orders marked delivered, by date and by courier".
- Fix: Add a paired requirement to FR-PAY-010 recording cash refunded against an order — amount, date, paying party, and method (cash, bank transfer) — and make it a valid refund type under FR-PAY-006 carrying a locally generated reference rather than a provider one. Require it to feed FR-PAY-008 as a negative so the report reconciles, and confirm it triggers a credit note under FR-ORD-021 like any other refund.

## [MAJOR] FR-ORD-009
**Exchanges have no state, and a rejected return has no exit from RETURNED**

- Problem: FR-ORD-009 promises "returns and exchanges", but the word exchange appears nowhere else in the document and Appendix A has no exchange path. Swapping a Ramadan title for another needs an inbound movement, an outbound movement, a price difference either way and a credit note — none exist. Separately, RETURNED is defined as goods "received and inspected", which implies inspection can fail, yet its only exit is REFUNDED. A box that comes back opened with cards missing, or arrives outside the policy window, has no destination; since FR-ORD-002 refuses unlisted transitions, the order is stuck in RETURNED with the stock already restored to sellable. Finally, the "policy window" that gates both RETURN_REQUESTED and the path to COMPLETED is defined by no requirement and is configurable nowhere — FR-CMS-004 versions the policy as prose only, so the enforced number and the published number can drift apart with nothing to reconcile them.
- Evidence: FR-ORD-009: "The system shall support returns and exchanges with a reason code, and shall restore stock on receipt of returned goods". Appendix A.1: "| RETURNED | Returned goods received and inspected | Restored |" and "| RETURN_REQUESTED | Customer has requested a return within the policy window | Unchanged until goods received |". Appendix A.2 lists only "| RETURNED | REFUNDED | Refund issued (FR-PAY-006) | Refund reference recorded |".
- Fix: Either drop "and exchanges" from FR-ORD-009 and state that an exchange is handled as a refund plus a new order — proportionate at this volume — or specify the path properly. Add an outcome for a rejected return (RETURNED to DELIVERED, or an equivalent state, with a mandatory reason and a decision on whether goods are returned to the customer or held). Add a requirement for a configurable return window in days from DELIVERED, referenced by both Appendix A rows that currently say "policy window".

## [MAJOR] FR-CRT-007
**Promotion codes are accepted but nothing creates, scopes or caps them**

- Problem: FR-CRT-007 is the only promotion requirement in the document. Nothing gives an Owner the ability to create a code, set a fixed or percentage value, an expiry, a minimum order value, a product or collection scope, a total redemption limit or a per-customer limit; section 4.1 has no sale-price or markdown mechanism either. The acceptance criterion itself refers to an "expired" code, a property no requirement ever confers. For a brand whose entire marketing channel is social posts to roughly 3,000 followers, the predictable failure is a launch code that gets screenshotted and reshared, redeems without limit, and can only be stopped by a developer. The omission also has effort consequences that a reader would currently miss: promotion management is a back-office area, not a checkout field.
- Evidence: FR-CRT-007: "The system shall accept a promotion code at checkout and shall display the resulting discount as a distinct line" | criterion: "A valid code shows a discount line; an invalid or expired code is refused with a reason."
- Fix: Add one Should-priority requirement for promotion code management covering value (fixed agorot or percentage), validity start and end dates, minimum order value, product or collection scope, total redemption limit and per-customer limit, and whether codes stack. Specify that a redemption is counted at order placement and released on cancellation. Also state whether the FR-ADR-008 free-delivery threshold is measured before or after discount and on the VAT-inclusive value — its criterion is precise to one agora but the basis is undefined.

## [MINOR] UI-006, NFR-USA-001, FR-ACC-010, NFR-SEC-003, FR-JRN-004, FR-JRN-021, FR-CUR-002, FR-RPT-012, FR-RPT-013
**Four requirement pairs state the same obligation twice under different identifiers**

- Problem: The same obligation is specified in more than one place, which breaches the single-statement principle in 29148 and creates maintenance traps: a change agreed against one identifier leaves the duplicate standing, and §7 verifies the same obligation under two groups with different instruments. UI-006 and NFR-USA-001 are the same WCAG 2.2 AA commitment with near-identical criteria, verified under both group 3.1 and group 6.5. FR-ACC-010's second sentence and NFR-SEC-003 both mandate server-side authorisation on every request, verified under group 4.7 (Test) and group 6.3 (Inspection + Test). FR-JRN-004 and FR-JRN-021 both assert scene linearity from AS-15, both Must. FR-CUR-002, FR-RPT-012 and FR-RPT-013 state the ILS-only display rule three times at two different priorities. §7's closing claim that 'every numbered requirement ... falls within exactly one group' is true of the identifiers but false of the obligations.
- Evidence: UI-006: 'The system shall meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA on storefront and back office interfaces'. NFR-USA-001: 'Storefront and back office shall conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA'. FR-ACC-010: '... Permissions shall be enforced server-side on every request'. NFR-SEC-003: 'Authorisation shall be enforced server-side on every request ...'. FR-JRN-004: 'The scene sequence shall be linear'. FR-JRN-021: '... shall not create divergent scene paths'.
- Fix: Keep one owning identifier per obligation and turn the other into an explicit cross-reference, exactly as UI-006 already does for the Journey. Suggested owners: NFR-USA-001 owns WCAG (UI-006 becomes 'Accessibility conformance is specified at NFR-USA-001'); NFR-SEC-003 owns server-side authorisation (strike the second sentence of FR-ACC-010, which should specify only the two-role model); FR-JRN-021 owns linearity, with its stronger criterion, and FR-JRN-004 is withdrawn as obsolete; FR-CUR-002 owns ILS-only presentation and FR-RPT-012 and FR-RPT-013 are withdrawn. Then correct the §7 coverage sentence to say that each obligation, not merely each identifier, is verified once.
